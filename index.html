Our story

Some places you visit.
Some places you remember.

REVOKO is an Indian hospitality and lifestyle company creating thoughtfully designed, experience-led destinations.

We bring together food, nature, design, community and memorable experiences to create complete places where people can slow down, reconnect and spend quality time.

Rather than thinking in terms of standalone venues, REVOKO treats the destination itself as the experience.
